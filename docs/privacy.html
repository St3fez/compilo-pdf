Privacy Policy

Compilo PDF · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short

Compilo PDF does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your phone and has no permission to use the internet.

What the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app stores the following in its private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it:

  • the PDF documents you open or import, with the changes you make;
  • the details you enter under “My details” (for example name, address, tax or ID number, phone, email);
  • the signatures you choose to save;
  • the values the app learns after you type them repeatedly in the same field;
  • technical files such as temporary copies used to print, share or convert a document.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can remove a document from the app at any time, forget learned values in “My details”, and delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Permissions and network

The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network. It does not request access to your photos, contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage: files reach the app only when you pick them with Android's file picker or share them with the app.

When you share or open a file with another app

If you choose Share, Open with, Save or Print, the file is handed to the app, printer or service you select. From that moment the policy of that app or service applies; Compilo PDF has no control over it and receives nothing back.

No third parties

Compilo PDF contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no component that collects data about you or your device.

Backups

Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off, so your documents and details are not uploaded to your Google account. Android may still copy them directly to a new phone if you use its phone-to-phone transfer.

Children

The app is not directed at children and, since it collects no data, it knows nothing about its users' age.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or erase on the developer's side. You are in full control of the data stored in the app. If you have questions about your rights under the GDPR or other laws, contact us using the link below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the date above changed before the new version is released.

Contact

Compilo PDF is developed by an independent developer. Questions about privacy can be sent through https://github.com/St3fez/compilo-pdf/issues.
