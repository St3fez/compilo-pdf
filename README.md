# Compilo PDF

Fill, sign and convert your PDFs on your phone. Offline, private, no account.

- **Fill forms faster**: finds the blanks in any PDF and fills the recurring ones from your details, in the document's own font and size.
- **Sign with your finger**: draw the signature once, save it, place it anywhere.
- **PDF to Word, offline**: editable Word documents with text, columns, tables and images in place.
- **Private by design**: no internet permission, no tracking, no ads. Cloud backup is off.

Available in Italian, English, French, German and Spanish.
